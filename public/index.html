⚖︎ Scope note — 2026 heavy obligations

The new 2026 CCPA duties (formal risk assessments, mandatory cybersecurity audits, ADMT notice & opt-out) are threshold-gated: they apply to businesses over ~$26.6M revenue, processing 100,000+ CA residents' data, or earning 50%+ of revenue from selling/sharing data. Most local clients don't trip these — leave them out of the deliverable unless the client qualifies.

CIPA, by contrast, is a private right of action with no size threshold. That's why the consent block is first.

Requirements also vary by industry — health, legal, and financial clients carry added disclosure and data-handling duties, so tailor the checklist before applying it.
